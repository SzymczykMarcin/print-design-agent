# Brand direction

Fictional demonstration identity. This direction supersedes the earlier restrained
multi-format example; it is not a commissioned or approved corporate identity.

## Character

Sunny, sociable, youthful. Show the pleasure of an everyday ice-cream break.
Combine a genuine human photograph with confident lettering and a few drawn marks.
Avoid invented product montages, testimonial claims and decoration competing with
the face, logo or headline.

## Palette

| Role | sRGB design reference |
| --- | --- |
| Cocoa: headline and footer | `#49281E` |
| Cream: open space and reversed lettering | `#FFF7EB` |
| Strawberry: expressive headline field and sticker | `#ED91A5` |
| Mint: small contrast accents | `#A4CDCA` |

These are source design references, not process-ink recipes. Convert the production
PDF into the printer's ISO Coated v2 (ECI) destination profile. Preserve the supplied
logo artwork; profile conversion for production is not a logo redesign.

## Typography

- Pacifico Regular: short expressive headline and invitation, never dense body copy.
- Bebas Neue Regular: the oversized word `LEPIEJ!`.
- Lato Regular: offer, address, hours and demo notice.

Bundled font licenses remain beside their files. All visible text must be converted
to curves by CorelDRAW. Exact text stays in `copy.md`; sizes, baselines and alignment
are reproducible in `working/compose_flyer.py`. Native text measurements are recorded
in `reports/typography-v02.json` before outlining. No hidden live-text duplicates.

## Logo and imagery

Use the supplied logo unchanged, with breathing room on cream. Do not redraw its
lettering, recolour its components or stretch it. The existing trace has small
irregularities; retain its authentic geometry.
Use the original photograph, preserving face, hand and ice cream together. Cropping
and vector overlays are permitted; generated replacement people/products are not.
The photograph, colour fields, logo and lettering remain separate source objects.
