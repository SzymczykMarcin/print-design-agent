# Lodziarnia brandbook

Proposed identity for this fictional example, not an official manual from the
logo owner. The existing mark says "LODZIARNIA" and "SMAK LEPSZYCH DNI"; preserve
that lettering as supplied. Campaign copy is English to match the public example.

## Character and voice

A friendly neighborhood stop for an uncomplicated ice-cream break. Warm, relaxed
and clear. Speak in short sentences about the experience. Avoid luxury language,
childish puns, exaggerated promises, health claims and unsupported origin stories.

## Logo

Use [the supplied vector](inputs/logos/lodziarnia.svg), already composed of paths.
Keep its proportions, colors and complete tagline. Do not reconstruct the lettering
with Lato. Its trace includes small irregularities; do not silently redraw them.

Place it on solid cream or white. Clear space around the visible mark: at least
one cap-height of the word LODZIARNIA, in addition to its built-in transparent margin.
Starting minimum visible-mark width: 50 mm in print or 320 px on screen; inspect
small lettering in the actual output. Measure visible artwork, not the SVG canvas.
Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, crop the tagline or place it over a busy photo.
No alternate logo versions are approved for this exercise.

## Palette

These are proposed companion colors inspired by the mark, not certified logo inks.
Retain the SVG's original fills. HEX and RGB below describe the digital palette;
CMYK values must be derived for the chosen printer profile, not guessed here.

| Color | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cream | #FFF6EC | 255, 246, 236 | Main background and breathing room |
| Cocoa | #49281E | 73, 40, 30 | Headlines, body and CTA text |
| Strawberry | #DF858D | 223, 133, 141 | Small panels, accents and rules |
| Vanilla white | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | Optional clean logo panel |

Start around 70% cream, 20% cocoa and 10% strawberry within non-photo areas;
this is a balance cue, not a layout formula. Use cocoa on cream for long text and
cocoa on strawberry for accent labels. Avoid small white text on strawberry.

## Typography

One family: **Lato**, with bundled Regular (400) and Bold (700). No fake bold,
extra decorative font or automatic substitution. The fonts include Latin and
Polish characters. Install/load these files in the chosen editor before layout.
License and provenance are recorded in `assets.md` and `inputs/fonts/OFL.txt`.

| Role | A5 starting recipe | Feed starting recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | Bold, 30 pt / 32 pt | Bold, 76 px / 80 px |
| Supporting copy | Regular, 12 pt / 16 pt | Regular, 34 px / 44 px |
| CTA | Bold, 16 pt / 20 pt | Bold, 42 px / 50 px |
| Visit details | Regular, 10.5 pt / 14 pt | Regular, 30 px / 38 px |

Use sentence case, left alignment and natural tracking. Adjust line breaks to the
actual width, not by shrinking the entire layout. For the story, start with the
feed sizes and reduce copy; inspect readability on a phone. Typeset, then outline
with CorelDRAW or Inkscape. Preserve exact copy in `copy.md` for future revisions.

## Photography and composition

Use the supplied lifestyle photo as a mood image, not a product photograph of
this shop. Keep natural skin and ice-cream colors; no beauty retouching or generated
replacement objects. Its warm pink details support the palette.

Start with a generous photo area and a separate cream area for message and CTA.
Give the headline a clear first-read position. Avoid ornamental blobs, excessive
badges and repeated rounded cards. Align copy blocks to a common left edge.

Crop separately for each format. Preserve face, mouth, cone and hand together;
use negative space around the subject without hiding the action. For story layouts,
keep key content away from interface overlays and verify the destination preview.
