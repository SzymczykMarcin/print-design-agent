# Design brief

## Purpose and audience

A fictional neighbourhood ice-cream shop invites young adults and friends to stop
for a relaxed summer treat. The flyer should feel warm, spontaneous and playful,
not corporate or like a product catalogue. One clear action: visit the shop.

## Scope

One single-sided A5 portrait flyer, 148 x 210 mm after cutting. Polish artwork;
English project documentation. No poster or social adaptations in this task.
Existing photo and vector logo are mandatory. No generative imagery.

## Direction

A close lifestyle crop, a cream shape suggesting a scoop, strawberry-pink headline
field and flowing cocoa footer. Pacifico handwriting contrasts with bold condensed
Bebas Neue; Lato keeps practical details readable. The supplied reference informs
energy, scale and image/type integration, not a layout to copy or claims to reuse.
The previous agent-authored brandbook is superseded by this direction.

## Demo facts and approval

Copy, address, hours and offer are fictional example data authored within the
user-authorized demo. They are not verified facts about a real shop and are not
approved for real advertising. A small printed demo notice remains in the flyer.
The photograph is a licensed lifestyle image, not a customer endorsement.

## Production target

User authorized choosing published printer requirements on 2026-09-20.
Target: Print24, A5, one-sided four-colour printing, 135 gsm coated art paper,
no folding or special finishes. See `reports/printer-specification.md`.
Document including bleed: 152 x 214 mm. Keep essential copy at least 5 mm inside
trim (project policy). Only a technically verified PDF belongs in `exports/print/`.
