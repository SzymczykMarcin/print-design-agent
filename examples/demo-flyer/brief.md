# Project brief: Green Corner

- Project name: a fictional plant-care workshop flyer.
- Goal and audience: encourage beginners in houseplant care to register.
- Offer / main message: an introductory plant-care workshop.
- Output formats: one-sided A5 flyer and a portrait social media graphic; exact social dimensions to be determined.
- Printer and production requirements: not selected; bleed and color profile require confirmation.
- Branding: no brand manual; provisional direction is green, cream, and restrained typography. Exact colors and fonts remain to be selected.
- Photographs and logos: a plant photograph and logo are needed in inputs/; neither is supplied in this example.
- Visual direction and references: natural photography, a clear headline, and minimal decoration.
- Required elements: workshop topic and registration details.
- Constraints and missing information: date, location, price, registration method, and graphic assets.
