# Project brief: Lodziarnia

Fictional demonstration brief. The supplied logo is an existing asset; the business
story, offer and address below are invented for this example, not verified facts
about its owner. Start with this file, then read `brandbook.md`, `copy.md` and
`assets.md`. Current progress is in `reports/status.md`.

## Purpose and audience

- Project: an everyday invitation to visit a neighborhood ice-cream shop.
- Goal: prompt an in-person visit during an afternoon walk.
- Audience: adults, friends and families looking for a small, affordable treat.
- Offer: vanilla or chocolate soft serve in a cone or cup. No discount campaign.
- Main message: a relaxed ice-cream break can brighten an ordinary day.
- Primary action: visit the shop; the address supports this action.
- Distribution: local flyer handouts and the shop's social feed.
- Language: English copy; preserve the existing Polish logo lettering unchanged.

## Inputs and direction

- Logo: `inputs/logos/lodziarnia.svg`, the existing VTracer no-cleanup result.
- Hero photo: `inputs/photos/ice-cream-moment.jpg`, a person eating soft serve.
- Fonts: bundled Lato Regular and Bold in `inputs/fonts/`.
- Brand system: `brandbook.md`; exact typeset text: `copy.md`.
- Direction: warm, approachable, photography-led; cream space for readable copy.
- Lead with the moment of enjoyment and headline, then the offer and visit details.
- Preserve the face, hand and ice cream when cropping. Keep copy off the busy photo.
- Do not imply that the stock subject endorses the shop or that the setting is its interior.

## Constraints and next step

No prices, testimonials, nutrition claims, QR codes or invented award badges.
Use the supplied logo without retracing or recoloring. Lay out real fonts and
convert the artwork text to curves; retain the text and style recipe in these files.

The completed v01 artwork and review evidence are linked from `reports/status.md`.
Copy the project outside the repository before creating a new campaign or revision. Business facts must be replaced and verified before real publication;
printer requirements must be obtained before technical print release.
